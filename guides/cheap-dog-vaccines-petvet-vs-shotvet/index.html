Cheap Dog Vaccines: PetVet (Tractor Supply) vs ShotVet

Last checked: November 18, 2026. Prices and hours change — call before you go.

Both chains run walk-in vaccine clinics at retail stores with no separate exam fee. For a single rabies shot, the published prices are within a dollar or two of each other. The bigger differences are where and when they operate and which bundle you buy.

What to know before you go

  • These are vaccine clinics, not full vet visits. ShotVet's own terms say the pet is examined to decide whether the chosen immunizations are appropriate and that it is not a full physical exam.
  • PetVet's FAQ says a dog's first rabies vaccine is valid for one year; later ones may be certified for three years if given on schedule. A lapse or missing records usually means a one-year certificate.
  • Bring prior vaccination records. Tag and registration fees are set locally and may be extra.
  • If your pet is sick, see a regular vet instead.
