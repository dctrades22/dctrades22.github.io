Low-Cost Vet, Vaccines and Spay/Neuter in Bainbridge, GA

Last checked: 2026-11-27. Prices and hours change — call before you go.

Short answer: for a cat, the cheapest published spay/neuter price in Bainbridge is the Bainbridge-Decatur County Humane Society’s program with Paws First: $75 per cat, plus $15 for rabies if your cat isn’t current ($75 total for community/feral cats, rabies included). For dogs, the humane society takes income-based applications for spay/neuter help for Decatur County residents but doesn’t publish a price, so apply or call (229) 246-0101. For walk-in vaccines, PetVet holds a Sunday-morning clinic about once a month at the Bainbridge Tractor Supply (dog or cat rabies $38, dog “Essential” package $84).

Bainbridge-Decatur County Humane Society (Bainbridge Animal Services)

Bainbridge-Decatur County Humane Society — 808 Zorn Road, Bainbridge, GA 39817 — (229) 246-0101 — info@bainbridgehumanesociety.com
Shelter hours listed: Monday–Saturday, 10 AM–5 PM (call to set up a time to meet a pet).
Stray, neglect or cruelty calls go to Animal Control, not the humane society: (229) 515-0172.

The shelter moved in 2024 from its old Cox Avenue building to the new Bainbridge Animal Services Center on Zorn Road, built with the City of Bainbridge and Decatur County. Older directories (including the United Spay Alliance list we cite on our Georgia page) still show the Cox Avenue address.

Cat spay/neuter with Paws First

The humane society partners with Paws First Mobile Veterinary Clinic for a cats-only spay/neuter clinic in Bainbridge, open to both owned and community (feral) cats. You book online through Paws First’s scheduler and pick the Bainbridge location. The humane society says appointments fill quickly.

Dog spay/neuter help (income-based)

For dogs, the humane society has an online “Spay/Neuter Assistance for Dogs” application. It asks for all household income and requires proof: a federal 1040 tax return or proof of state/federal benefits (Social Security, unemployment, SSI, a medical card, etc.) or pension benefits. W-2s alone are not accepted. The form asks whether you live inside city limits or in the county, and it authorizes the society to verify your income. No dog price is published, so call for the current price. The society’s donations page says it offers spay/neuter vouchers to Decatur County residents, runs a TNR program for community cats, and occasionally holds free spay/neuter events for county residents (no dates posted).

Free microchips (yearly event)

According to a May 2026 WTXL (ABC 27) report, the humane society gives away 25 free microchips every year around May at its Justin Bedwell Microchip Memorial Event, and plans to open a waitlist for the next one. Call to ask how to get on it.

Paws First Mobile Veterinary Clinic

Paws First holds spay/neuter, vaccine and wellness clinics around South Georgia. Its list includes Bainbridge and the Lake Seminole area. Some clinics run with shelters or rescues, so booking may go through them. Contact: (229) 504-5409, admin@pawsfirstvet.com. These are Paws First’s general prices. The humane society’s Bainbridge cat price is above.

Drive to Thomasville: South Georgia Low Cost Spay Neuter

For a dog when the humane society has no help available, the nearest nonprofit clinic we found that posts prices is in Thomasville (425 Covington Ave; (229) 236-7297): cats $70, dogs $90–$135 by weight, add-on vaccines and microchip $15 each, plus a $30 non-refundable deposit applied to your bill. Its site doesn’t list a service area, so call to confirm it takes Decatur County pets. Details are on our Thomasville low-cost vet page.

Sick or injured pet / after hours

The humane society, Paws First and PetVet don’t treat emergencies. We found no 24-hour emergency hospital in Bainbridge that takes new patients.

  • If you’re already a Bryan-Hight client, it says it’s on call 24/7 for active clients: (229) 246-8600. During business hours, Bainbridge Animal Hospital asks you to call first: (229) 515-3784.
  • Allied Veterinary Emergency Hospital, 2324 Centerville Rd, Tallahassee, FL — (850) 222-0123. Its site advertises 24/7 emergency care.
  • Northside Animal Hospital, Valdosta (3398 N Valdosta Rd; (229) 244-2983), lists 24/7 emergency services.

None of these publish emergency prices. For payment plans and charity help with a big bill, see emergency vets and help paying a vet bill.
