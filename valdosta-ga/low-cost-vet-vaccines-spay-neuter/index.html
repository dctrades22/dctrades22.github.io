Low-Cost Vet, Vaccines and Spay/Neuter in Valdosta, GA

Last checked: November 20, 2026. Prices and hours change — call before you go.

Short answer: the cheapest published option in Valdosta is the Humane Society of Valdosta/Lowndes County clinic: spay/neuter is $25 for cats and $35 for dogs, and that price includes a rabies shot, a distemper-type vaccine and a microchip. For shots only, the same group runs a monthly vaccine clinic (dog distemper/parvo and cat HCP listed as free, rabies $10), and PetVet at the Valdosta Tractor Supply holds walk-in Sunday clinics. Book early: the humane society says its surgery slots fill fast and require a deposit.

Adopting? Lowndes County shelter fees include the basics

Lowndes County Animal Services (337 Gil Harbin Industrial Blvd, Valdosta; (229) 671-2760) charges $80 for dogs and $55 for cats. Every adopted pet is spayed or neutered and gets a vet exam, rabies shot, microchip and first set of vaccines. Dogs over 6 months are heartworm tested. Adoption hours listed: Mon and Sat 12–3 PM, Tue–Fri 12–4 PM. For after-hours animal emergencies (strays, bites) the county says to dial 911.

Sick or injured pet

The options above are spay/neuter and vaccine clinics, not full-service hospitals, so call a regular vet for a sick or injured pet. South Georgia Low Cost Spay Neuter says its technician “wellness days” are for simple boosters only, with no exam and no sick pets. For urgent problems, Northside Animal Hospital (3398 N Valdosta Rd; (229) 244-2983) says on its website that it offers full emergency services 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Prices are not published, so call. For payment plans and grants, see emergency vets and help paying a vet bill, which also covers another Valdosta 24-hour hospital.

Older listings online (including some third-party directories) describe an earlier humane society voucher program with different prices and a different phone number. The prices above come from the humane society's current program pages as of Nov 20, 2026. If you see a conflict, trust the clinic's own page or a phone call.
