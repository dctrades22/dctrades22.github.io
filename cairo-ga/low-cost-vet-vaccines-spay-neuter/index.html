Low-Cost Vet, Vaccines and Spay/Neuter in Cairo, GA

Last checked: 2026-11-28. Prices and hours change — call before you go.

Short answer: Cairo has no low-cost spay/neuter clinic of its own. The local help is Best Friends Dog Rescue, which Grady County says offers income-based help to spay or neuter dogs and cats. It doesn’t publish a price, so call (229) 377-1538. The closest nonprofit clinic we found that posts prices is South Georgia Low Cost Spay Neuter in Thomasville: cats $70, dogs $90–$135. For stray or feral cats, Grady County Animal Control says it will trap them (or help you trap them), get them sterilized at no cost to you, and return them. For walk-in vaccines, PetVet holds a Sunday-afternoon clinic about once a month at the Cairo Tractor Supply (dog or cat rabies $38, dog “Essential” package $84).

Spay/neuter help: Best Friends Dog Rescue

Best Friends Dog Rescue — 1330 Bold Springs Rd, Cairo, GA 39827 — (229) 377-1538 — bestfriendsdogrescue@windstream.net
Visits by appointment. Call before you go.
  • Grady County’s animal control page says Best Friends Dog Rescue gives income-based help with the cost of spaying or neutering cats or dogs.
  • The rescue says it focuses on low-income parts of the community. Its national partner listing adds that it helps low-income families with both the cost and transportation.
  • No price, income limit or application is published. Call and ask what you would pay and what proof of income they need.
  • The rescue also runs adoptions for the county shelter. Its dogs are vet-checked, vaccinated and spayed/neutered before adoption. No adoption fee is published.

Don’t mix up the names: Best Friends Dog Rescue is a local Cairo nonprofit founded in 2001. Best Friends Animal Society is a separate national group that has given grants to Grady County programs.

Grady County Animal Control (stray, lost and feral cat help)

Grady County Animal Control — 421 Livestock Pavilion, Cairo, GA 39828 — (229) 377-3070
Hours listed: Monday–Friday, 8 AM–5 PM. No walk-in drop-offs. Call for an appointment to surrender a pet. For an animal in immediate danger, call 911.

The county says its shelter is now no-kill and works with Best Friends Dog Rescue, which handles vet care, spay/neuter, shots and adoptions or transfers to other rescues.

A 2021 Best Friends Animal Society article (third-party) says Grady County community cats trapped for this program are taken to South Georgia Low Cost Spay Neuter in Thomasville, then spayed or neutered, vaccinated and returned the next day.

Sick or injured pet / after hours

Best Friends Dog Rescue, animal control and PetVet don’t treat emergencies. We found no 24-hour emergency hospital in Cairo.

  • Cairo Animal Hospital’s emergency line, (229) 220-1592: call to see if it can take your pet. It doesn’t say whether it sees non-clients after hours.
  • Allied Veterinary Emergency Hospital, 2324 Centerville Rd, Tallahassee, FL — (850) 222-0123. Its site advertises 24/7 emergency care, and Animal Medical Center sends after-hours emergencies there.

Neither publishes emergency prices. For payment plans and charity help with a big bill, see emergency vets and help paying a vet bill.
