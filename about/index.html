About and Affiliate Disclosure

Low Cost Vet Georgia is an independent, one-person project that collects published prices, hours and eligibility rules for low-cost pet care, beginning in Albany and Southwest Georgia. We link the original source for every number, show the date we checked it, and do not accept payment for listings.

Affiliate disclosure

We plan to earn money from affiliate and referral links (for example pet-supply retailers or pet insurance programs). As of November 18, 2026 this site contains no affiliate links. If we add any, the link will be labeled on the page where it appears, and it will not change what we list or how we rank clinics. Clinics cannot pay to be listed higher.

Accuracy

Prices and hours change. We are not veterinarians and this is not medical advice. Confirm with the clinic before you travel. To report an error or suggest a clinic, open an issue on GitHub.

Low Cost Vet Georgia is an independent guide. Not veterinary advice; in an emergency call the nearest emergency vet. See our affiliate disclosure and privacy policy.
