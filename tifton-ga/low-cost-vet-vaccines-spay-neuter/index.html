Low-Cost Vet, Vaccines and Spay/Neuter in Tifton, GA

Last checked: 2026-11-25. Prices and hours change — call before you go.

Short answer: Tifton has no stand-alone low-cost spay/neuter clinic that publishes prices online (as of our check). The cheapest published routes are: SpayGeorgia certificates, which two Tifton vets (Quailwood and Branch's) have been honored for accepting; the walk-in PetVet clinic at the Tifton Tractor Supply about once a month on Saturday afternoons (dog or cat rabies $36, dog “Essential” package $79); and Tift County Animal Control, which offers free microchips with Tift Animal Rescue and says it can point you to the most cost-efficient way to spay or neuter. If you are adopting, the county shelter's fee ($75 cat / $85 dog) already includes the spay/neuter surgery and core shots.

Spay/neuter options for Tifton pets

1. SpayGeorgia certificates (used at local vets)

SpayGeorgia sells low-cost (not free) spay/neuter certificates for Georgia residents who need partial financial help. A certificate covers a routine spay or neuter (prep, anesthesia, surgery, routine hospital stay, stitch removal); vaccines, disease tests and deworming are extra. How it works:

  • Request the information packet online, or call (800) 706-7081 and leave your name and address to get it by mail. The packet has the current prices and the list of participating vets — prices are not published on the website.
  • Mail the order form with payment (certificates can't be bought online). SpayGeorgia asks for an optional $3 donation toward postage.
  • Certificates expire six months after they are issued and can't be combined with other discounts.
  • When you book, ask the clinic which vaccines it requires before surgery and what it charges for them — you pay for those.

Two Tifton practices have been recognized by SpayGeorgia: Quailwood Animal Hospital was its Veterinarian of the Year for 2024 (SpayGeorgia says Quailwood does hundreds of surgeries for certificate holders each year), and Branch's Veterinary Clinic – Tifton shared its 2023 Clinic of the Year award. Check your packet's vet list to confirm either one is still participating before you book.

2. Tift Animal Rescue

Tift Animal Rescue is a volunteer 501(c)(3) that supports the county shelter and says it also helps fund spay/neuter, vaccines and microchipping for community pets. Fix Georgia Pets lists it as the spay/neuter partner for Tift County, with contact by email only: tiftrescue@yahoo.com. No prices or event dates are published — email to ask about upcoming spay/neuter events.

3. Drive to Thomasville: South Georgia Low Cost Spay Neuter

The nearest nonprofit clinic we found that posts its prices is in Thomasville (425 Covington Ave; (229) 236-7297). Its site does not list a service area, so call to confirm it will take Tift County pets.

Sick or injured pet / after hours

The PetVet clinic and the spay/neuter options above don't treat sick or injured pets.

  • Quailwood asks you to call ahead for emergencies during business hours. After hours, call its main number, (229) 386-8794, and follow the prompts; it says someone will call you back. It describes its emergency care as limited.
  • Branch's says to call (229) 382-6055 to see whether it can fit your pet in for an emergency.
  • Northside Animal Hospital in Valdosta (3398 N Valdosta Rd; (229) 244-2983) says it offers full emergency services 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

None of these publish emergency prices. For payment plans and charity help with a big bill, see emergency vets and help paying a vet bill.
