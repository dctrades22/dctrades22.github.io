Low-Cost Vet, Vaccines and Spay/Neuter in Thomasville, GA

Last checked: 2026-11-21. Prices and hours change — call before you go.

Short answer: Thomasville has two nonprofit spay/neuter options with published prices. South Georgia Low Cost Spay Neuter charges $70 for any cat and $90–$135 for dogs depending on weight, and Thomasville Humane's Wellness Waggin' lists cat neuters at $70, cat spays at $90 and dog surgeries from $125. For a rabies shot only, Thomasville Humane lists $15 and says it holds low-cost rabies clinics during the year (dates are not posted, so call). The walk-in PetVet clinic at the Thomasville Tractor Supply runs about once a month on Sunday afternoons, with dog rabies at $38.

Cheap vaccines in Thomasville

Rabies shots: Thomasville Humane

Thomasville Humane lists a rabies vaccination at $15 and says it hosts low-cost on-site rabies clinics throughout the year. Upcoming dates are not published on its site; call (229) 228-0613 (listed hours: every day, 1–6 PM). It also lists microchipping at the shelter for $25.

Booster shots: South Georgia Low Cost Spay Neuter “Tech Wellness Days”

On select days the spay/neuter clinic offers technician-only visits for vaccine boosters (dog DAPPv and cat HCPCh series). These visits have no vet exam, no rabies shots and no sick or injured pets. You book online and pay a deposit based on the services you pick; the wellness-day page does not list prices, so check the booking screen or call.

Adopting: fees include spay/neuter

Thomasville Humane (no-kill) lists usual adoption fees of $60 for adult cats, $120 for kittens, $120 for adult dogs and $200 for puppies. Some long-stay or special-needs pets cost less or nothing, and some high-demand pets cost more under its “Legacy” pricing. The fee covers spay/neuter, a microchip, required vaccines and deworming if needed.

Thomas County Animal Services (run by the Sheriff's Office; 270 Joiner Rd; (229) 236-2772) also adopts out animals, Monday–Friday 8 AM–5 PM. Its page does not publish adoption fees — call.

Strays, surrenders and community cats

  • Thomasville Humane says it stopped providing animal control on January 1, 2023 and cannot accept strays. Stray, loose or injured animals go to Thomas County Animal Services; after hours, the county says to call dispatch at (229) 225-4151 (officers handle loose-animal and stray calls the next business day) and 911 for emergencies involving serious injury.
  • County Animal Services does not take owner-surrendered pets. Thomasville Humane takes surrenders by online application only; it says review can take several weeks, admission isn't guaranteed, and the intake fee is $200 per pet.
  • Feral cats: Thomasville Humane rents humane traps for a $70 refundable deposit, and its Wellness Waggin' lists TNR at $50. South Georgia Low Cost Spay Neuter's TNR page says resources are “coming soon”; its regular $70 cat price applies to feral cats too.

Sick or injured pet / after hours

The spay/neuter and vaccine clinics above don't treat sick pets. Thomasville Animal Hospital (1103 Smith Ave; (229) 226-4561) says it handles emergencies during business hours, but its after-hours line (229-338-7313) is for existing clients only — weeknights before 10 PM and 8 AM–5 PM on weekends and holidays. At other times it points people to two fully staffed emergency hospitals:

  • Allied Veterinary Emergency Hospital, 2324 Centerville Rd, Tallahassee, FL — (850) 222-0123; its site advertises 24/7 emergency care.
  • Valdosta Veterinary Associates — (229) 834-8387 (number as listed by Thomasville Animal Hospital).

Northside Animal Hospital in Valdosta (3398 N Valdosta Rd; (229) 244-2983) also says it offers emergency services 24/7. None of these publish emergency prices, so call before you drive. For payment plans and charity help, see emergency vets and help paying a vet bill.

All prices above come from each clinic's or agency's own website as checked on Nov 21, 2026; we did not use third-party directories for any number on this page. Fee pages can lag behind real prices, so confirm when you book.
