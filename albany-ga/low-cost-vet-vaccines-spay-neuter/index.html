Low-Cost Vet, Vaccines and Spay/Neuter in Albany, GA

Last checked: November 18, 2026. Prices and hours change — call before you go.

Short answer: for spay/neuter, the lowest published prices in Albany are at East Albany Animal Clinic. For routine dog and cat vaccines, there is a monthly walk-in PetVet clinic at the Albany Tractor Supply, and Albany Humane Society says to contact them for low-cost vaccination options.

Spay/neuter: East Albany Animal Clinic

  • Fees include a microchip with lifetime registration.
  • Extra charges may apply for dogs in heat, pregnant or obese, and for umbilical hernia repair.
  • The discounted fees are income-qualified: bring printed proof (most recent federal tax return or 3 most recent pay stubs), or be referred by a vet for affordability reasons, or be a shelter/rescue, or bring a feral cat in an approved trap. Discounts do not apply to new puppies/kittens or planned breeding.
  • Call to book. Note (2026-11-25): the clinic’s old web address (eastalbanyac.com) now shows an unrelated site, so we have removed the link and could not re-confirm these prices online — confirm by phone.

The clinic is a registered nonprofit whose stated mission is a low-cost spay & neuter program and preventive care for pet owners in need; it reports working in Dougherty County (GuideStar profile).

Albany Humane Society

Albany Humane Society (1705 W Oakridge Dr, Albany, GA 31707; (229) 888-7387; appointment only per its website) says on its animal admission page: “Please contact us for Low Cost Vaccination options or Cat Food Assistance.” We could not find published vaccine prices, so call for current options.

Other options near Albany

  • Statewide certificates: SpayGeorgia sells low-cost spay/neuter certificates redeemable at participating vets. It is not free, certificates expire six months after issue, and vaccines are not covered. Request the information packet to see participating clinics.
  • Bainbridge: the Bainbridge-Decatur County Humane Society and Paws First list a $75 cat spay/neuter, and PetVet holds a monthly Sunday vaccine clinic at the Bainbridge Tractor Supply; see low-cost vet, vaccines and spay/neuter in Bainbridge.
  • Cairo (Grady County): Best Friends Dog Rescue offers income-based spay/neuter help, animal control traps and sterilizes feral cats at no cost to you, and PetVet holds a monthly Sunday vaccine clinic at the Cairo Tractor Supply; see low-cost vet, vaccines and spay/neuter in Cairo.
  • Valdosta: the Humane Society of Valdosta/Lowndes County lists much lower spay/neuter package prices; see low-cost vet, vaccines and spay/neuter in Valdosta.
  • Thomasville: South Georgia Low Cost Spay Neuter and Thomasville Humane's Wellness Waggin' both publish spay/neuter prices, and Thomasville Humane lists $15 rabies shots; see low-cost vet, vaccines and spay/neuter in Thomasville.
  • Tifton: PetVet holds a Saturday vaccine clinic about once a month at the Tifton Tractor Supply, and Tift County Animal Control offers free microchips; see low-cost vet, vaccines and spay/neuter in Tifton.
  • Moultrie: the Moultrie-Colquitt County Humane Society's SNIP program lists $35 cat and $62.50–$70 dog spay/neuter, and PetVet runs a Saturday clinic at the Moultrie Tractor Supply; see low-cost vet, vaccines and spay/neuter in Moultrie.
We have not been able to verify prices or openings at every Albany vet. If a clinic should be listed, tell us via GitHub issues.
