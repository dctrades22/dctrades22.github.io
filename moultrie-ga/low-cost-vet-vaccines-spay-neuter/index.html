Low-Cost Vet, Vaccines and Spay/Neuter in Moultrie, GA

Last checked: 2026-11-26. Prices and hours change — call before you go.

Short answer: the cheapest published spay/neuter prices in Moultrie come from the Moultrie-Colquitt County Humane Society's SNIP program (Anne's Fund): $70 for a female dog, $62.50 for a male dog (Colquitt County residents only) and $35 for a cat, with a rabies shot included. The same humane society lists $15 rabies and free DAPP and feline distemper shots through a partner vaccination clinic. For walk-in vaccines without an appointment, the PetVet clinic at the Moultrie Tractor Supply runs about once a month on Saturday afternoons (dog or cat rabies $36, dog “Essential” package $79).

Moultrie-Colquitt County Humane Society (shelter and animal control)

Moultrie-Colquitt County Humane Society — 1412 1st Street NE, Moultrie, GA 31768 — (229) 985-5463 (ext. 1 front desk, ext. 2 animal control, ext. 3 director)
Listed hours: Tuesday–Saturday, 9:30 AM–12:00 PM and 1:00–5:30 PM.
Email: mcchumanesociety@gmail.com; spay/neuter questions: moultriespayneuter@gmail.com

The humane society is a nonprofit that also handles animal control under contract. According to an August 2026 Moultrie Observer report, Colquitt County and the City of Moultrie have contracts, but the smaller cities in the county did not, and the society said it would stop picking up animals in those cities (except dangerous-dog calls) until contracts were signed. If you live outside Moultrie's city limits but inside a town, ask whether pickup applies to you.

Spay/neuter: SNIP program (Anne's Fund)

The humane society runs this year-round program with the Humane Society of Valdosta and Paws First Mobile Vet. The humane society collects payment, checks eligibility and schedules the appointment.

In a 2023 Moultrie Observer story, the shelter said the program was income-based and asked applicants to bring a valid driver's license and their most recent tax return; the current page doesn't list documents, so ask what to bring.

Paws First Mobile Veterinary Clinic

Paws First is a mobile clinic that holds spay/neuter, vaccine and wellness clinics around South Georgia (it lists Moultrie, Pavo, Thomasville, Tifton, Sylvester, Bainbridge and others) and is the SNIP program's vet partner. Book through its online scheduler. Some clinics run with shelters or rescues, so you may have to book through them. Contact: (229) 504-5409, admin@pawsfirstvet.com.

The surgery page says Paws First only takes dogs up to 65 lb and under 7 years old, though it also lists a 66–85 lb neuter price, so ask if your dog is over 65 lb. Prices include a 24-hour pain injection; budget an estimated $20–$30 more for an e-collar and take-home pain medication. Pets not current on rabies get a rabies shot for $15 more.

Drive to Thomasville: South Georgia Low Cost Spay Neuter

If the SNIP program has no openings, the nearest nonprofit clinic we found that posts its prices is in Thomasville (425 Covington Ave; (229) 236-7297): cats $70, dogs $90–$135 by weight, add-on vaccines and microchip $15 each, plus a $30 non-refundable deposit applied to your bill. Its site doesn't list a service area, so call to confirm it takes Colquitt County pets. Details are on our Thomasville low-cost vet page.

Sick or injured pet / after hours

The SNIP program, Paws First and PetVet don't treat emergencies. We found no 24-hour emergency vet in Moultrie.

  • During its hours (Mon–Fri 7:30 AM–5:30 PM), call Southside at (229) 985-7377 for urgent care or phone triage.
  • Matthews' emergency care page lists: Allied Veterinary Emergency Hospital, Tallahassee ((850) 222-0123); Valdosta Veterinary Associates ((229) 834-8387); and OnPoint Veterinary Urgent Care, Tallahassee ((850) 807-6607; urgent care open daily 10:00 AM–10:00 PM, not 24 hours). Matthews says to call ahead to make sure they're open and can treat your pet.
  • Northside Animal Hospital in Valdosta (3398 N Valdosta Rd; (229) 244-2983) says it offers full emergency services 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

None of these publish emergency prices. For payment plans and charity help with a big bill, see emergency vets and help paying a vet bill.
